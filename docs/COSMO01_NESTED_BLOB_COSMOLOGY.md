Some terminology overlaps published research, but no single derivation unifies the drawings. This ledger separates **source claims**, **published physical hypotheses**, **mathematical abstractions**, and **measured synthetic results**.

The scientific target is not to validate those drawings by visual similarity. It is to ask whether **a bounded observer misses internal differences that become consequential under permitted interactions**, and only later ask if any fully specified physical theory produces testable signatures.

### Three relation levels that must not be conflated

1. **Mathematical:** an edge and update law connect variables.
2. **Causal within the specified simulation:** intervening on an edge or state produces a changed counterfactual output under fixed rules.
3. **Physical spacetime:** a metric and field equations support a causal structure compatible with physical constraints and observations.

Passing level 1 or 2 does not imply level 3.

## COSMO-01 E0: predeclared, falsifiable exact toy test

**Research question:** Does a coarse observer of two bounded systems fail to predict a future pair of aggregates, even when individual states and dynamics are fully specified? When a gated interdomain junction is allowed, does the same model change any outputs?

**Unit:** two four-pocket binary domains, A and B. One-step synchronous majority update with path/cycle intradomain edges, and an optional connection A3-B0. Every one of 256 initial bit patterns receives uniform weight.

**Coarse observer:** \`M=(sum(A),sum(B))\`. **One-extra-bit observer:** \`(M,A3)\`. **Target:** \`Y=(next sum(A),next sum(B))\`.

**Intervention modes:** isolated; fixed permitted bridge; adaptive bridge only when \`sum(A)>sum(B)\`, subject to three independently enforced boolean eligibility gates: permission, contact, compatibility. This asymmetric adaptive rule is chosen as a transparent toy control, not a proposed principle of cosmology.

**Metrics:** complete-state witness where M agrees but Y differs, H(Y|M), H(Y|M,A3), additional predictive information, bridge activations, between-mode differences; all topology/mode cells published. Use this definition of observational collision only in the *toy model*.

**Nulls and falsifiers:**
1. No-gate variants must behave identically to isolation for all 256 states.
2. Identity dynamics must yield exactly zero hidden predictive entropy.
3. The refined observation must not increase conditional entropy.
4. Graph wiring and update rule must be disclosed; no number without its assumptions.
5. Zero, tie or regression remains a published result; no selective presentation.
6. No route to production automation, neural inference, hardware, operator permissions, or physical hypothesis promotion from E0.

**What E0 cannot tell us:** E0 contains no spacetime metric, gravitational field, energy-momentum tensor, Einstein/Einstein-Cartan equations, brane action, quantum state or CMB prediction. Its binary majority dynamics need not conserve energy, mass, or charge. Calling an E0 bridge a "wormhole" would be misleading.
