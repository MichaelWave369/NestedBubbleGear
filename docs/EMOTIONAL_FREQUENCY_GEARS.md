# NBG-EFG1 | Emotional Feedback and Frequency-Gear Coupling

**Status:** PROPOSED research note, not a frozen protocol or completed experiment. **Date:** 2026-10-20. **Epistemic origin:** literature citations = SOURCE-ATTRIBUTED; NBG analogy = INFERRED; EFG1 model and predictions = PROPOSED. **Empirical authority:** NONE. **Execution:** none; no EFG1 results, human measurements, or validated effects.

## Question

Can emotional state and interpersonal feedback explain *incremental, out-of-sample* collective coordination or persistence beyond shared schedules, common events, ordinary network structure, and non-emotional behavioral coupling?

This proposes a **social/agent-based model**, not a cosmological or physical account of the universe. It does not posit an invisible reservoir of emotional energy, literal human-frequency gears, supernatural control, or a detectable Demiurge. Such ideas may be discussed as philosophical metaphors but have no evidential status in this track.

## Testable hypothesis and competing explanations

**EFG-H1 (incremental emotion):** Given matched social graph, common drivers, schedules, stochasticity, and model capacity, adding correctly specified interpersonal emotional feedback improves predeclared held-out predictions of future aggregate behavior compared with a strong baseline that does not receive interpersonal emotional state.

**EFG-H0 (parsimonious null):** Shared stimuli, schedule coordination, non-emotional interaction, autocorrelation, and existing network constraints explain the observations sufficiently. An emotional-feedback feature adds no replicable predictive or causal value.

**EFG-H2 (toothwork):** Prespecified interface constraints (thresholds, delays, saturation and permitted edges) alter propagation and stability beyond equally parameterized unconstrained-coupling baselines, under interventions on those constraints.

**EFG-H3 (policy revision / Spark analogy):** Allowing a subset of agents to revise a decision rule after receiving new information changes measurable cascade, resilience, or norm-persistence outcomes relative to a capacity-matched non-revising policy.

**Important:** If synthetic data are *generated by* a model containing an emotion term, fitting that term is a positive-control recovery exercise, **not** independent evidence that emotions cause real-world social coordination. Synthetic ground truth can only validate methods and expose failure modes. Any empirical interpretation would require separately sourced, consented data and causal discrimination.

## NBG mapping and observational Keyholes

- **K0 | Surface alignment:** coincident behavior, shared commuting patterns, correlated sentiment or repeated events. High risk of confusing common input with transmission.
- **K1 | Common causes:** common schedules, environmental events, public messages, external shocks, and independent agent autocorrelation.
- **K2 | Interaction history:** actual edges, direction, timings, exposure opportunities, response lags, and selection into networks.
- **K3 | Internal-state proxy:** time-resolved, explicitly defined emotion proxies; reliability and missingness must be modeled. Written sentiment is **not** a direct readout of subjective feelings.
- **K4 | Counterfactual intervention:** randomized synthetic edge removals, driver perturbations, shuffled connections, constraint changes, and policy switches; prospective real-world studies require independent ethical review.

An aggregate synchrony trace at K0 may be observationally compatible with multiple distinct histories. A proposed NBG-style test asks whether a richer lawful Keyhole separates histories and improves a **specified future prediction**. Without an identified distinguishing intervention, do not claim causal recovery.

## Why a coupled-oscillator model is useful, but insufficient alone

One candidate timing submodel is a graph-normalized Kuramoto-style equation:

```text
theta_i(t+1) = theta_i(t) + dt * [
  omega_i + (K / max(1, degree_i)) * sum_j A_ij sin(theta_j - theta_i)
]
```

Here `theta` is the phase of a **declared repeated behavior**, not an emotional or consciousness frequency. Emotion is a separate candidate state `e_i(t)` and should enter a stated behavioral policy or interaction mechanism. To make an emotional claim, compare matched models with/without that pathway. Alternative non-oscillator baselines (e.g. time-of-day and network autoregression) are mandatory; a sine curve should not be rewarded simply because we called the process a gear.

## The social-machine / Demiurge analogy

The metaphor of a society reproducing its own constraints through habit, conformity and institutions may illuminate philosophy, sociology, and fictional narratives. The analogy does **not** establish that a supernatural Demiurge designed the world, that a simulation exists, or that physical energy is being harvested from emotion. NBG-EFG1 concerns human-scale information, motivation, choices, network dynamics and falsifiable simulated behavior only.
