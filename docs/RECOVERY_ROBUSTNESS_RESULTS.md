## Interpretation boundaries

- **Horizon rescue:** for 18 distinct base configurations at least one perturbation location is censored by 38 s but satisfies the recovery definition by 120 s. This is a direct counterexample to treating finite-horizon nonrecovery as a permanent fact **in the toy model**.
- **Threshold sensitivity:** eight base configurations change their 38-second recovered/censored classification when the allowed residue threshold changes. Conclusions require an explicit observer rule.
- **Kick-site classification:** no **binary** classification changed across A/B/BOTH at the selected 38 s, threshold 0.06 and dt 0.04 setting. That does **not** imply identical trajectories, recovery times, or no effect of perturbation location.
- **Step-size classification:** no recovered/censored status flip was seen at the selected 38 s, B kick and threshold 0.06 when dt went from 0.04 to 0.02. This is *not* a general numerical convergence proof.
- **Keyhole mismatch:** 328 of 1,458 threshold-scored outcomes combine high baseline undisturbed velocity correlation with no observed return of the kicked full state. Correlation remains a distinct coarse observer, not a resilience proxy.
- **No numeric runaway** in the selected positive-damping set does not establish stability of every possible parameter set or nonlinear dynamics.

The BR3 panel is intentionally a **27-case subset of BR2's parameter space**, with additional off-BR2-grid values (such as 0.85 coupling and 0.07 damping). Therefore, BR3 counts are not directly additive to BR2's 972 cells, and the matching count of 328 in the two sweeps is not evidence that they identified the same configurations.

There are no observations of natural systems, people, consciousness, theology, physical gears or chaos here. All findings are explicitly scoped to the chosen dimensionless synthetic linear oscillator model.
