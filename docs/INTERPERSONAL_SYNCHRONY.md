# NBG-IS1 | Interpersonal Synchrony and Field Hypotheses

**Status:** RESEARCH NOTE / PROPOSED. **Date:** 2026-10-20. **Evidence origin:** bibliographic sources VERIFIED; descriptions SOURCE-ATTRIBUTED; NBG mappings INFERRED; experimental hypotheses PROPOSED. **NBG empirical authority:** NONE. **No observations, participants, field measurements, or model results were produced by this note.**

## Question and boundary

Can a richer, properly controlled observer distinguish genuine cross-person information transfer from the appearance of synchrony caused by shared sensory input, behavioral coordination, within-person autocorrelation, or sensor artifact?

This question belongs in Nested Bubble/Gear because a coarse *Keyhole* (such as simultaneous pulse changes or a subjective feeling of connection) can conflate different causal histories. A richer Keyhole may distinguish them. That statement is a **research analogy**, not evidence that NBG or a brain-body system possesses an unmeasured electromagnetic communication mechanism.

NBG-IS1 is separate from the existing NBG-RB electromagnetic/bioelectric **regeneration** program, from NBG-BR synthetic oscillator recovery, and from any claims about consciousness or treatment. It changes none of their frozen datasets, methods, checksums, gates, or conclusions.

## Keyhole map (analogical, not yet fitted to data)

Consider person A, person B, the ambient environment, and the measurement system as **distinct** coupled or possibly independent subsystems. Unknown input must remain unknown rather than being silently assigned to a field.

- **K0 | Impression:** self-reports of rapport, shared timing, or the feeling of tension. Vulnerable to memory, expectation, and cue-reading.
- **K1 | Behavioral/environmental:** timestamps, task events, voice, movement, gaze and other lawful, consented sensory/common-input measures. These can often explain synchrony.
- **K2 | Physiological:** time-aligned ECG/PPG-derived interbeat intervals, respiration and, if independently justified, EEG/fNIRS. Autocorrelation, respiration, movement, common task timing and preprocessing must be modeled.
- **K3 | Physical-field/instrument:** calibrated biomagnetic measurements, ambient noise, instrument cross-talk, sensor placement, distance and geometry. A recorded signal is not automatically received or decoded biologically.
- **K4 | Causal discrimination:** preregistered randomized or otherwise justified controls that isolate a proposed pathway, while preserving ethics and realistic physical exposure conditions.

The *same* K0/K2 correlation could arise under independent people responding to shared sound, ordinary conversation, sensor leakage, statistical overfitting, or an additional real coupling mechanism. NBG's precise contribution would be a disciplined **observer-comparison problem**: when does an additional lawful observation improve out-of-sample discrimination, and is it uniquely predictive beyond conventional alternatives?

### Competing explanations, not an assumed conclusion

- **C0, common-driver null:** observed alignment is adequately explained by recorded/unrecorded common inputs, sensory communication, within-person dynamics, and measurement artifacts.
- **C1, interaction-specific coordination:** cross-person history improves held-out predictions after measured common inputs; could still be ordinary behavioral or sensory coupling.
- **C2, field-mediated signaling:** an identified, independently calibrated physical field channel makes an incremental, mechanistically credible, causally discriminated contribution beyond C0/C1.
- **C3, shared-consciousness interpretation:** not inferable from C2 even if C2 were supported; separately undefined until operational predictions distinguish it.

C1 is not C2. C2 is not C3. An apparent lag, frequency match, phase coincidence, or correlation by itself establishes none of them.
