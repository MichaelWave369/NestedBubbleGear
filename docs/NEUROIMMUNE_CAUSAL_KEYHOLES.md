# NBG-NI1 | Neuroimmune Feedback and Causal Keyholes

**Research status:** PROPOSED E0 literature and model-design note (2026-10-25). **NBG origin:** INFERRED / PROPOSED; literature descriptions SOURCE-ATTRIBUTED. **Empirical authority:** NONE. There is no NI1 fit, human sample, wet-lab experiment, clinical intervention, or evidence of nonlocal consciousness effects.

## The actual question

Can bounded observations of a multi-compartment brain–autonomic–endocrine–gut–immune system conceal distinct causal histories that would become distinguishable under an admissible future perturbation and a richer Keyhole? Could those differences be explained using established physical signaling and delays, without postulating remote effects on isolated biological samples?

This is a *model comparison and observability question*, not a health treatment claim or a proof that an NBG abstraction describes biology.

## Evidence ladder and scope

| Ben-Shaanan et al. (2016), Nature Medicine | Experimentally activating reward-system VTA neurons in mice altered monocyte/macrophage antibacterial function and bacterial load; sympathetic-system perturbation reduced part of the effect. | Direct neural intervention in a living mouse, *not* separated blood responding to thoughts. |
| Koren et al. (2021), Cell | In mice, reactivating experimentally tagged insular ensembles restored aspects of prior inflammation. | Neural encoding of immune-associated state; not human conscious recall or memory at a distance. See 2021 methods erratum. |
| Jin et al. (2024), Nature | Defined vagal sensory and brainstem pathways participate in bidirectional inflammatory regulation in mice. | Circuit-specific causal study, not a universal vagus "healing frequency." |
| Stress-sensitive neural circuits (2024), Cell | Mouse amygdala–vagus–Brunner gland pathway altered Lactobacilli and infection susceptibility. | An organ- and context-specific pathway, not proof that mood instantly controls bacteria in a detached drop. |

## Physical causal pathways: explicit carriers matter

Conventional candidate edges include:
- brain reward/arousal circuits -> sympathetic activity -> peripheral immune cell trafficking/function;
- inflammation-derived cytokine signaling -> vagal sensory neurons -> brainstem circuits -> autonomic output;
- stress circuits -> neuroendocrine signaling (including HPA-axis hormones) -> immune response;
- brain/autonomic activity -> gut gland secretion / microbiota environment -> host defense;
- neural representation of prior inflammation -> physiological state reactivation in experimentally manipulated mice.

These interactions operate on different timescales and can have context-dependent directions. Greater inflammation is **not** necessarily healthier immunity.

## NBG map

Let compartments be bubbles with local latent state `x_i(t)`, interfaces `Sigma_ij`, allowed physically named transfers `T_ij`, and Keyhole observations `y_i(t)=P_i[x_i(t)]+epsilon_i(t)`. The illustrative discrete evolution is:

```text
x_i(t+Δt) = f_i(x_i(t), u_i(t),
                 Σ_j T_ji(x_j(t-τ_ji), Sigma_ji),
                 epsilon_i(t))
y_i(t)    = P_i(x_i(t)) + measurement_noise_i(t)
```

No universal units, universal frequencies, real parameter values, field amplitude, or biological validity follow from this symbolic family.

- **Bubbles:** brain, sympathetic/vagal pathways, endocrine, tissue immune cells, gut lumen, external isolated sample, microscope/readout.
- **Paths:** measured or explicitly simulated physical transport or receptor-mediated communication.
- **Gears:** defined physiological feedback circuits, with delays and state dependence; not literal teeth.
- **Keyholes:** K0 mood + one immune readout; K1 stress hormones/autonomic measures and tissue context; K2 direct pathway intervention; K3 independent sample handling and acquisition controls.
- **Altermath test (formal, conditional):** construct two hidden states `X` and `X'` with `P(X)=P(X')`. Only call this an NBG witness if a predeclared admissible future operation `w` produces `P(rho(w)X) != P(rho(w)X')`. A matching cartoon is not evidence that real immune systems instantiate this witness.
- **History/memory:** distinguish ordinary biological history dependence, neuron-tagging evidence, and speculative NBG memory analogies; no assignment of subjective consciousness to a macrophage.

For a sealed, physically isolated sample under a *conventional causal model*, **there is no brain-to-sample signal path after separation**; continued changes are attributed to the sample's stored initial state, chemical environment, cell interactions, handling, and measurement apparatus. A proposed nonlocal edge must *not* be quietly added to the null.

## Specific nonlocal hypothesis firewall

The social "family immunity" extrapolation adds an entirely separate person-to-person transmission claim. It belongs in NBG-IS1's hypothetical field-vs-common-cause framework, not among established NI1 anatomical pathways. No shared DNA is a demonstrated remote signaling mechanism. A lack of observed conventional signal would be insufficient to validate a novel channel: experiments also require calibration, independent blinding, shuffled timing, sample-vial swapping, sham donors, environmental covariates, and multiple-testing control.
