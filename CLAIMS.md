# Claim Firewall

NBG separates **demonstrated toy-model results**, **formal work under development**, and **speculative interpretation**.

## Demonstrated in frozen finite toy models

- Latent hidden-state revelation after an initially identical observation.
- Boundary-transferred causal residue.
- Observable return without full-state return.
- Order dependence under noncommuting interface composition.
- Closed-loop hidden residue under a frozen commutator sequence.
- Orientation-dependent loop residue with exact inverse cancellation.
- Two-plaquette local nontriviality with exact global outer-boundary cancellation.

These are exact properties of the published/executable constructions. They are not empirical claims about nature.

Controls, revocation and forward/commit identity audits passed. The reviewed result is `FAIL_W1G_GATE_ALIGNMENT`. No compression claim is made.

## Rule

A successful toy-model benchmark may justify the next mathematical question. It does **not** by itself upgrade a speculative physical interpretation into evidence.
