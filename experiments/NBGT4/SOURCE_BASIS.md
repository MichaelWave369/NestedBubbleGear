# NBG-T4 Source Basis

## Motivating artifact

`timeline-hidden-history.pdf`, supplied in the working conversation.

The PDF identifies the project as the **Deep State Mapping Project** and describes the **Q-web** as a chronological timeline. Its introduction says the center timeline is divided into topic categories, arrows indicate a connection of interest between keywords, many topics are instead placed beside adjacent topics without an arrow, and the map intentionally blends generally accepted facts with plausible theories while asking viewers to use discernment.

Those source-defined semantics are exactly why the artifact is useful as a hostile-ingest design target: chronology, arrows, adjacency, density, and certainty level cannot safely be treated as one generic graph edge.

## Redistribution policy

The PDF/image itself is not copied into this repository.

NBG-T4 uses a **small synthetic derivative fixture** containing abstract node names and synthetic locators. The fixture reproduces structural failure modes only. It does not reproduce the map's full wording, layout, or claim network.
