The natural next rung is a three-plaquette chain or L-shaped complex.

2. Can two parenthesizations agree at the full operator level?
3. What breaks if an intermediate connector is omitted?
4. Which conjugacy invariants survive a global change of basepoint?
5. Can a coarse observer see identical total invariants while missing distinct transported decompositions?
