# AH22 Frozen Specification

## 1. Purpose

AH22 computes finite Pareto frontiers over policy hardening choices.

For candidate policy \(\mathcal P'\), freeze three objectives:

1. minimize added authorization cost;
2. minimize policy-induced singleton cut count;
3. maximize diagnostic reliability at \(p=0.1\).

No scalar score is used.

## 2. Candidate family

For each context:

\[
\mathcal P\subseteq\mathcal P'\subseteq\mathcal A,
\]

with \(\mathcal P'\) upward-closed and compliant with any hard deny constraints.

## 3. Cost

Each newly authorized coalition \(C\) has declared cost \(w(C)\ge0\).

\[
\operatorname{Cost}(\mathcal P')=
\sum_{C\in\mathcal P'\setminus\mathcal P}w(C).
\]

Costs are toy governance weights, not dollars or legal risk estimates.

## 4. Pareto dominance

Candidate \(A\) dominates candidate \(B\) iff:

\[
\operatorname{Cost}(A)\le\operatorname{Cost}(B),
\]

\[
S_{\rm induced}(A)\le S_{\rm induced}(B),
\]

\[
R_A(0.1)\ge R_B(0.1),
\]

and at least one inequality is strict.

The Pareto frontier contains every nondominated legal candidate.

## 5. Full-task contexts

### H2_FULL

Baseline minimal coalition: \(\{E_1,E_2\}\).

Optional capable coalition:

\[
\{E_2,E_3\},\qquad w=3.
\]

Expected frontier:

| added cost | induced singleton cuts | reliability |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 1 | 0.810 |
| 3 | 0 | 0.891 |

### GLOBAL_REAUTH

Same topology, but freeze:

\[
w(\{E_2,E_3\})=5.
\]

Expected frontier:

\[
(0,1,0.810),\quad(5,0,0.891).
\]

### ROUTE_REAUTH

Optional capable coalition:

\[
\{E_1,E_2\},\qquad w=2.
\]

Expected frontier:

\[
(0,1,0.810),\quad(2,0,0.891).
\]

## 6. Alarm soft-cost context

Baseline policy is the AH20 alarm policy generated by minimal authorized coalition \(\{E_3\}\).

No hard deny is active.

New coalition costs:

\[
w(\{E_1,E_2\})=1,
\]

\[
w(\{E_1\})=4.
\]

Because upward closure of singleton \(\{E_1\}\) also adds \(\{E_1,E_2\}\), full mathematical capability costs:

\[
5.
\]

Expected legal policies and frontier points:

### Baseline

\[
(\text{cost},\text{induced cuts},R)
=
(0,1,0.900).
\]

### Pair-backup hardening

Authorize only new pair \(\{E_1,E_2\}\):

\[
(1,0,0.981).
\]

### Full capability

Authorize singleton \(\{E_1\}\), which also requires its upward closure:

\[
(5,0,0.990).
\]

All three are expected to be Pareto-nondominated.

## 7. Alarm hard-deny context

Freeze:

\[
\{E_1\}\text{ forbidden}.
\]

The full-capability point is now illegal rather than merely expensive.

Expected frontier:

\[
(0,1,0.900),
\qquad
(1,0,0.981).
\]

This context proves that a hard deny changes the feasible set, while a high cost leaves a policy feasible but potentially unattractive.

## 8. Reliability

Use the same diagnostic toy model as AH20/AH21: each Keyhole independently fails with common probability \(p\).

At \(p=0.1\):

- full-task baseline: 0.810;
- full-task capability: 0.891;
- alarm baseline: 0.900;
- alarm pair hardening: 0.981;
- alarm capability: 0.990.

## 9. Target reliability queries

For the alarm soft-cost frontier, freeze:

- minimum cost achieving \(R\ge0.98\): 1;
- minimum cost achieving \(R\ge0.99\): 5.

For the hard-deny alarm context:

- \(R\ge0.98\): cost 1;
- \(R\ge0.99\): infeasible.

## 10. Interpretation

AH22 supports:

\[
\boxed{\text{governance tradeoffs should be exposed as a frontier, not hidden in one score}}
\]

and distinguishes:

\[
\boxed{\text{expensive}\neq\text{forbidden}}.
\]

## 11. Claim firewall

AH22 does not establish real governance costs, correct risk preferences, deployment recommendations, legal/compliance weights, or safety-critical reliability. It enumerates the frozen finite trade space under declared toy weights and the AH20 failure model.
