## Provenance policy

For each source:

- bibliographic existence may be marked VERIFIED;
- reported experimental observations remain attributed to that source;
- NBG does not silently relabel source-reported observations as independently reproduced facts;
- interpretation added by NBG is INFERRED unless separately demonstrated;
- synthetic controls are SIMULATED.

## Corpus freeze rule

Before execution, RB1 must replace this seed ledger with a frozen corpus manifest containing the exact source set, inclusion / exclusion rule, extraction date, field-level extraction provenance, duplicate-lineage map, source-lineage split, and content hashes / receipts where legally and technically appropriate.
