# AH24 Candidate — Heterogeneous and Correlated Failure Regimes

AH23 finds no frontier membership reversal under the frozen common-identical-failure uncertainty set.

AH24 should relax the symmetry assumption.

Candidate regimes:

- E1-fragile: `(p1,p2,p3)=(0.30,0.10,0.05)`
- E3-fragile: `(0.05,0.10,0.30)`
- common-cause failure involving E1/E3
- common-cause failure involving mandatory E2

Questions:

1. Can policy reliability rankings reverse across heterogeneous regimes?
2. Does Pareto membership change when coalition identities face different hazards?
3. Can one policy be robust to one regime and brittle to another?
4. How should scenario uncertainty be exposed without assigning fake probabilities?
5. Can correlated-failure controls distinguish true redundancy from duplicated failure domains?
