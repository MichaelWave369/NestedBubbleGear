# NBG-CW1 — Cosmic-Web Keyhole Closure

**Status:** protocol candidate only. No empirical NBG result is claimed by this rung.

NBG-CW1 is the first controlled physical-application track for Nested Bubble/Gear. It asks whether progressively richer observers of simulated and observed cosmic-web filaments preserve enough state for future prediction.

The governing question is:

> If two cosmic-web states are indistinguishable through a frozen Keyhole now, can admissible later evolution reveal that discarded microstructure was behaviorally relevant?

## Claim firewall

Morphological resemblance between the cosmic web and NBG diagrams is **not evidence for NBG**.

CW1 can only promote an NBG observer if it demonstrates predictive or closure value beyond simpler baselines under frozen criteria. A negative result is a valid outcome.

## Frozen conceptual mapping

- **Bubble:** a bounded halo/CGM or other locally modeled domain.
- **Gear:** the effective structured coupling or transport law across/between domains.
- **Keyhole:** a frozen observational projection.
- **Latent state:** simulation-resolved density, temperature, ionization, velocity, dark-matter and related fields.
- **Closure failure:** present-day Keyhole equivalence followed by future Keyhole divergence.
- **Refinement:** adding the minimum additional observable state needed to recover predictive closure.

The physical filament is not automatically called a Gear. The filament is substrate; the Gear is the effective interface/transport structure represented by the model.
