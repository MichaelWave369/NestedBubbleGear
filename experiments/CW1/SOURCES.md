# NBG-CW1 Source Ledger

This file records the public scientific starting points for the CW line. It is a source ledger, not a claim that the papers endorse NBG.

## Primary observational anchor

Davide Tornotti et al., **“High-definition imaging of a filamentary connection between a close quasar pair at z = 3”**, *Nature Astronomy* 9, 577–588 (2025).

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41550-024-02463-w
- Published: 29 January 2025
- System: MUSE Ultra Deep Field, quasar-host pair at approximately (z=3.22)
- Relevance to CW1: directly detected Ly-alpha filament emission, CGM/IGM transition measurement, longitudinal/transverse profiles and comparison with cosmological simulations.
- Data note: the paper states that the underlying VLT data are available from the ESO archive.

## Ensemble observational anchor

Davide Tornotti et al., **“Cosmic web Ly-alpha emission in a sample of overdense regions”**, arXiv:2609.20938 (submitted 17 September 2026).

- Record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20938
- Scope: 41 overdensities over roughly (3 \lesssim z \lesssim 5) in MUDF and MXDF.
- Relevance to CW1: detections and non-detections across environment density, projected emitting filling fractions, and inferred dense filament spines/substructure.

## Use policy

CW1 protocol files may cite quantitative results from these sources with attribution.

This repository does **not** copy third-party figures, article text, proprietary simulation snapshots or observational products into CW1 merely because they are scientifically convenient. Data ingestion must follow the source license/archive terms and receive its own provenance receipt.

## Claim discipline

These sources establish that high-redshift cosmic-web gas can be studied through direct Ly-alpha emission and simulation comparison.

They do not establish NBG, Gear dynamics, Altermath, or any fundamental-spacetime interpretation.
