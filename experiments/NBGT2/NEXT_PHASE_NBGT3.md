# NBG-T3 Candidate — Contradictory-Source Reconciliation

NBG-T2 freezes typed temporal relations so chronology, allegation, documentation, inference, and contradiction cannot silently collapse into one generic arrow.

NBG-T3 should freeze **source reconciliation semantics** without pretending uncertainty can always be reduced to one score.

Candidate invariants:

1. supporting and opposing evidence may coexist;
2. duplicate copies of one source do not count as independent corroboration;
3. source independence must be represented explicitly rather than inferred from different URLs or filenames;
4. an unresolved conflict remains `DISPUTED` when qualification criteria are not met;
5. later evidence changes only later knowledge snapshots;
6. provenance is preserved through any confidence/status update;
7. `REFUTED` requires an explicit frozen rule and cannot be produced merely because one side has more records;
8. confidence summaries are derived views, not replacements for the underlying evidence ledger;
9. counterfactual source removal remains separate from observed-history reconciliation;
10. source-order and duplicate-order changes do not alter canonical reconciliation output.

A later hostile-ingest rung can then feed a dense historical map into this machinery while keeping every claim, source, contradiction, and uncertainty state inspectable.
