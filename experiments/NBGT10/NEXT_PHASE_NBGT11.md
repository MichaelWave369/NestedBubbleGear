# NBG-T11 Candidate — Policy Ledger + Temporal Governance Keyholes

NBG-T10 qualifies explicit trust-policy outcomes and conflict-resolution receipts.

NBG-T11 should make governance itself temporal and inspectable.

Candidate scope:

1. append-only policy registry history;
2. policy valid-time and known-time;
3. policy amendment / supersession receipts;
4. no-hindsight governance replay;
5. side-by-side Governance Keyholes;
6. resolution receipts pinned to the policy version actually used;
7. later policy changes never rewrite earlier resolutions;
8. stale-policy detection across historical cutoffs;
9. explicit emergency-policy activation/deactivation;
10. portable governance bundle export and deterministic replay.

The point is not to discover a universally correct policy. It is to make policy evolution as provenance-safe as evidence evolution.
